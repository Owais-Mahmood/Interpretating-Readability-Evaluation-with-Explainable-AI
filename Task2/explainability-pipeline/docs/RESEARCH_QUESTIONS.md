# Reusable research questions

1. Do explanations localise decision-relevant units?
2. Do interventions confirm the claimed importance?
3. Do explanations align with human rationales or observed edits?
4. Are explanations stable across small changes, seeds, and checkpoints?
5. Are explanations target-class specific?
6. Are they consistent across languages and aligned examples?
7. Where do methods disagree, and which evidence resolves disagreement?
8. What value is gained for the additional computational cost?
